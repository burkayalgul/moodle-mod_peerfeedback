# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — question text

- The first line of a question is its title; further lines are shown beneath it with their line breaks.
- The list of people to give feedback to wraps onto new lines instead of scrolling sideways.
- The student list on the groups page is alphabetical, shows each student's email (when the site shows it to teachers), and has a search box for name or email. Ticks are kept across searches, so you can pick students from several searches and assign them at once.
- New "Everyone alone" button: each student gets a group of their own, named after them.
- "Pair" and "Alone" apply to the ticked students only when some are ticked; other groups stay as they are.
- The "Random…" panel opens over the page instead of pushing the toolbar buttons apart.
- Teacher feedback for students: each student has a "Teacher feedback" list on the activity page with the teacher's
  comment (and grade, when grading is on) on every feedback they wrote, shown once the teacher marks it reviewed —
  now also when grading is off.
- Overall comment: on the review page the teacher can write one comment about all the feedback a student wrote, save it
  as a draft, show it to the student, or hide it again.
- Students get a Moodle notification when a commented or graded feedback is marked reviewed, and when an overall comment
  is shown to them.
- The review page lists only students who are in a group, group by group, and has a group filter.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

- First release: activity-owned groups (assign, random, pair), eight survey-style question types, answering screen with
  progress, four student-visibility modes, optional self-evaluation, teacher review with grade/comment/lock, gradebook
  average, export by giver or receiver, completion rule, backup/restore and Privacy API.
