# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — question text

- The first line of a question is its title; further lines are shown beneath it with their line breaks.
- The list of people to give feedback to wraps onto new lines instead of scrolling sideways.
- The "Random…" panel opens over the page instead of pushing the toolbar buttons apart.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

- First release: activity-owned groups (assign, random, pair), eight survey-style question types, answering screen with
  progress, four student-visibility modes, optional self-evaluation, teacher review with grade/comment/lock, gradebook
  average, export by giver or receiver, completion rule, backup/restore and Privacy API.
